# Conquest and shared world picture — v18

The existing diplomatic executor now accepts two unilateral free-text actions: administer occupied land and annex controlled land. Both use actual province control and reject unoccupied or still contested targets. Neither requires the former government's signature or a third power's recognition. The existing map ownership, economy and saved-state mechanisms apply the result. An annexation keeps a disputed political claim; it does not guarantee cooperation or remove outside opposition.

Full occupation identifies a displaced government. It may communicate, negotiate and seek help, but cannot apply tax, spending or internal legislative measures to institutions it no longer controls. Tax collection follows the GDP share of territory under actual control. Installing administration adds one expense line to the existing budget; there is no separate occupation interface, integration timer or mandatory checklist. Full absorption ends the former state's ordinary cabinet operation and stops its unfinished recruitment. Undeployed enemy soldiers are never gifted to the conqueror.

All cabinets receive one shared compact picture of active wars, cumulative occupied and annexed gains, victims' lost share of starting production, actual troops abroad, known battle losses and disputed annexations. Each cabinet also sees its own exposed neighbors, partners and border forces. Changes in province control notify interested governments and major powers; unchanged facts do not endlessly repeat the same notice. The model chooses pressure, assistance, agreements, preparation, intervention or reasoned neutrality through existing political actions. No country-specific coalition, mandatory declaration of war or additional recurring model call is scripted.

The existing newspaper remains the presentation of confirmed decisions and results. Technical statuses stay in order details. A former cabinet's claim to sovereignty cannot be described as restored territorial control without an actual change on the map.

## Verification

Free integration checks cover unilateral and partial annexation, displaced-government powers, tax collection, budget identity, save/reload, repeated mandates, foreign context and control loss. Existing institution and amphibious tests run alongside the new module. Desktop and touch Chromium verify an administration-plus-annexation mandate through the ordinary turn flow.

Two separately bounded live campaigns are used for this task: ten turns as an expanding power and ten as a government trying to preserve regional balance. Their saved decisions, receipts, newspaper, usage and state are the evidence for the playtest report. Live workflow is one-shot and is archived after completion; ordinary CI never invokes paid models.
