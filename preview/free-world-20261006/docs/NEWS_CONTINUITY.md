# News continuity, political responses and guest portrait trial

Entry: economy-world.html?v=2. Branch: experiment/news-continuity.
Existing political-world and orders entries are preserved.

## Confirmed coverage
Every submitted order gets a visible article for acceptance, beginning execution, or a stated obstacle. All player articles remain visible; the former three-article cap no longer replaces the newspaper with a small subset. Delayed execution, completed missions and recruitment milestones are covered from confirmed engine events.
Multiple recruitment progress notices in one skipped period consolidate to the latest milestone; completion supersedes preliminary milestones. Immediate tasks and their official decision article share one identity. Foreign completions have distinct completion headlines, not a second announcement of the same plan.

Foreign news has up to seven distinct confirmed articles and an expandable archive of additional real events. Seven is a capacity, not a requirement to fabricate news.

## Political continuity
Diplomatic and political actions create addressed persistent issues. Recipients are prioritized in subsequent political selection, and the issue, prior signals, court circumstances and recent published headlines enter model context.
Responds_to identifies the question being answered. If omitted, an explicit reverse-addressed action can link to a matching pending issue using both counterpart and content. Unrelated decisions never close an issue solely because the same country acts.
Condition_actor can express an immediate response to another country's decision in the same batch. Dependencies are ordered; a sender that did not act cannot produce a purported response.

Repeated warnings, condemnation, negotiation, support or opposition without new material context are not re-executed or republished. Stance and motives remain in actor memory. New deployments, a changed regime or a changed domestic policy can justify a new action. Waiting accepts empty article fields: no-action is a valid decision and is not advertised as an event.

## Resilient orders
Planner requests use short O1/O2 identifiers, mapped back to permanent IDs. Unknown entries are ignored; consistent duplicates execute once; conflicting or absent orders remain deferred.
One bounded repair request is allowed when the model omits orders or omits all foreign decisions. It requests only missing orders and actor coverage, and does not deduct another game turn. If repair also fails, valid orders proceed and missing orders remain queued. The engine never infers execution from missing content.
Schema errors affect that individual order rather than aborting an otherwise valid turn. Material application failures still use atomic rollback.
Harmless category mismatches are normalized only when all fields unambiguously belong to an existing typed operation. Explicit recruitment durations are retained.

## Personal politics
Typed narrative court_scene stores participants, their roles, description, optional health condition and duration. Characters persist across turns and are included in model context.
These circumstances support conversations and personal political continuity; they cannot create soldiers, money, titles or arbitrary death. The timeline records circumstances ending; illness can enter recovery without declaring immediate complete recovery.

## Portrait trial
Server guest-ai v7 allows one successful manual guest portrait for the server's shared testing allowance, with at most one provider-failure retry. It is not one free image for every newly created anonymous identity.
The claim is atomic and service-role-only. Ordinary client roles cannot reset or claim it directly. Existing custom anonymous authentication remains required; shared OpenRouter keys remain server-side.
No portrait was generated by the developer during verification; the successful trial remains for the user's manual click.

## Education and relief
Both are contractual monthly expenditures accrued daily. Actual service delivery decreases when the treasury cannot pay, and inflation decreases real spending power.
Education spending per estimated school-age child influences gradual literacy change. School-age children remain a documented 28% approximation.
Relief is currently allocated to workers and peasants, contributes to their real disposable income, and influences poverty and mortality estimates. This is a simplified eligibility model, not an individual means test.
The UI shows per-child and per-recipient amounts, including a warning about population approximation.
